Who it applies to. Irregular-hours and part-year workers, such as cover cleaners, for leave years starting on or after 1 April 2024.

How it builds up. At the end of each pay period, holiday equals 12.07% of the hours actually worked in that period. For example, 40 hours worked gives 4.83 hours of holiday.

Why 12.07%. The statutory 5.6 weeks of leave, divided by the 46.4 weeks a year when a worker is not on leave.

Taking leave. Staff can only take holiday they have already earned. This tracker stops a booking that is bigger than the balance.

Rolled-up holiday pay. Employers may instead pay 12.07% on top of each payslip, shown on a separate line. If Crystal does this, the leave must still be recorded here when it is taken.
